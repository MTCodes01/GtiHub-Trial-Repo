# Contribution Guide for Commit Overflow

Welcome to the Commit Overflow event! This guide is tailored for participants looking to make their first (or fiftieth) contribution to this repository.

## Getting Started

1. **Find an Issue**: Look for issues labeled `good first issue`, `beginner`, or `help wanted`. Ensure no one else is currently assigned to it.
2. **Claim the Issue**: Comment on the issue asking to be assigned. Wait for a maintainer or mentor to assign it to you before starting work.
3. **Follow the Workflow**: Follow the Fork Workflow outlined in our [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

## What We Expect

- **Quality over Quantity**: Take your time to write clean, understandable code.
- **Ask for Help**: If you get stuck, tag a mentor in the issue thread. We are here to help you learn!
- **Follow the Templates**: Use the provided issue and PR templates strictly. Automated checks will reject your PR if they are not followed.

## Commit Overflow Specific Rules

- Every PR must link to a valid issue.
- Do not submit PRs for typos unless they are part of a larger documentation overhaul.
- Respect the mentors and maintainers. The Contributor Covenant applies to all interactions.

Good luck, and happy hacking during Commit Overflow!
